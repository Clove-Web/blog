Honestly, this is a 5am post, and I'm unable to sleep due to stuff being no my mind. A lot of poeple think I was faking it, and this is kinda how I was coping.

## So, how did I get here?
Well, in my previous post, about my life (TW if you look), you would have seen I disowned my parents. It wasn't easy, and this is one of 2 reasons why I was homeless. The other being the general housing situation and how screwed the local councils are.

I originally went homeless in 2024, but was lucky my partners at the time put a roof over my head and kept me safe. I still thank them all, even if some hate me now, for everything they did for me. I sought housing, I was looking for council help, and there was nothing really I could do.

## Why didn't I private rent?
Losing my parents meant I didn't have a guarantor, my income wasn't enough to put down a decent deposit, and I was just, struggling with the income for it. I would find a place, think I struck gold, and either it was taken, some being same day, or in cases, they wouldn't take me as I'd be using my benefits to pay the rent.

## So, what happened while I was on the streets?
Good question to have, and it depends which time you meant, as I've been street homeless twice now.

The first time, I slept overnight in a hostel, which wasn't... safe... and sadly ended up overdosing on medication, and admitting myself to A&E. I went to the council the following morning, sleep deprived and just annoyed at everything. I explained I was fleeing abuse and that I was a vulnerable person (due to my CPTSD), and sadly, ran out of the day to finish up, and they were closed til after the weekend. They made an appointment for me on the Monday, and I would be back in to get everything working.

I slept a few nights on the street, before my guardian saw I was on the street, and booked me a private student accom til that Monday morning. From there, the council contacted my GP, with my consent, booked me into a hotel that night, and asked to come back again the next day. Overnight, my GP confirmed, I was a vulnerable adult, and they stuck me in that hotel until I was able to start University, which was... lonely and I had moments of depression, and lost one of my (now ex-)partners in the process, but ultimately I was able to survive.

The second time, I was homeless with my girlfriend, and I was lucky to have my friend keep us both a month, while we searched for help, and got... nothing. We also (due to my gf and my friend clashing all the time) ended up making our stay shorter lived. When we got back to my GF's town, we ended up sleeping in her car for a few days, and both being physically exhausted. We asked her council for support, and while they could support my girlfriend, they couldn't support me. I was ready to face the streets again, or go back to my old town, when we finally came to an agreement with her nan.
